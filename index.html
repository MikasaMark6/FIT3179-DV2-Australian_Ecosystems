Sources & methods

How to interpret the vegetation data
  1. Display: approximately 5 km cells, sampled from the NVIS 100 m raster. Small coastal patches may disappear at this scale. Map cells are not used to calculate areas. Extant data have different observation dates across regions.
  2. Area statistics use original 100 m NVIS cells in an equal-area projection. One cell equals one hectare. Percentages in tooltips use all 25 classified native groups nationwide as the denominator.
  3. Each percentage divides the group's area by all 25 classified native groups in that state. It does not measure the percentage of the state's total land. The ten displayed groups are a subset; their percentages do not sum to 100% per column. Other Territories are omitted.

DCCEEW: NVIS Version 7.0 data products — extant MVG raster. Administrative boundaries: ABS ASGS Edition 4. See the processing notes for denominators and map simplification.

This section is a vegetation comparison, not a complete ecosystem inventory. Cleared, aquatic, unclassified and other non-comparison classes remain visible on the map but are excluded from the 25-group comparisons.
